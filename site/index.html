Research note · Dry bulk freight

Tonne-Mile Demand Model

Moves in the Baltic Dry Index are decomposed into four measurable forces: how much cargo moved, how far it sailed, how much fleet was available, and how much of that fleet was tied up in port, plus two public price signals that track the commodity cycle. What none of them explains is shown as a residual, never hidden.
